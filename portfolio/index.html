03 — About

The next shift in cybersecurity will not come from another dashboard. It will come from reliable AI-assisted execution.

I have spent almost twenty years in networking, cybersecurity, technical presales, and security architecture — working with partners, vendors, and customers across the Czech enterprise market, for both multinational and local companies.

Today I am building TIA Enterprise: an AI-native cybersecurity and intelligence company focused on agentic workflows, evidence-driven security analysis, OSINT, application security review, and human-governed automation.

The work sits at the intersection of cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, governance, and operational execution. I design systems where AI agents can assist with research, analysis, reporting, remediation, and decision support without losing traceability, accountability, or human control.

The goal is simple: tools and workflows that make security work faster, clearer, more defensible, and more useful for real organizations.
