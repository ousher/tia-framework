WHAT IS NOT ON THIS PAGE
  • No public client case studies. Not because there is no client work — because we do not publish a client without their written consent, and we have not asked. That is a real gap in what a buyer can verify, and it is ours to close by asking, not by writing around it.
  • No benchmark of our own product against competitors. A comparison table drawn by the vendor is a leaflet. If we publish one, it will contain the rows where we lose.
  • The mechanism behind the frameworks stays internal. The principle spreads; the implementation does not. We would rather say that than imply everything is open.
  • Our own capability inventory is not public. It lists what we cannot do as carefully as what we can, which makes it a shopping list for a competitor and a map for an attacker.
